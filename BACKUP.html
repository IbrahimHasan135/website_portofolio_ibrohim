About Me

I am not just an IoT Engineer — I am a Systems Thinker who decodes reality through hardware, material science, and structural analysis.

With a background spanning mechatronics, embedded systems, and full-stack development, I build end-to-end solutions — from circuit design and control systems to cloud infrastructure and interfaces. But the underlying discipline never changes: model the system, isolate its material constraints, then engineer a working solution.

My broader thesis is that engineering, science, and social analysis share one language — materialism. Just as a firmware bug traces back to a physical constraint, I hold that social and philosophical questions trace back to material conditions. That conviction sits behind both my hardware work and the research and essays published alongside it.

As the founder of Novalynk, I channel this systems approach into building scalable IoT products — turning material constraints into technology that works.
