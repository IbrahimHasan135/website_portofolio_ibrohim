About Me

I am an Industrial IoT & OT Systems Engineer focused on embedded firmware, industrial gateway development, and automation system integration.

With 1 year of embedded system experience, I work across ESP32-based firmware, PCB design, sensor integration, OTA update, REST API, MQTT, Zigbee, Modbus, and field troubleshooting for real industrial environments.

My work connects hardware and operational technology: developing multi-protocol gateways, building real-time data pipelines, and integrating field devices with backend systems so monitoring and control can run reliably at scale.

I am also trained in PLC, HMI, and Modbus, with project experience in climate control systems, Ferbos Gateway firmware, smart door lock integration, smart irrigation, and other microcontroller-based R&D projects.
